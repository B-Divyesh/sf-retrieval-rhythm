Privacy

Effective August 28, 2026

Your practice stays local

Retrieval Rhythm stores collections, prompts, answers, timing, and review history in your browser’s IndexedDB storage. We do not receive that information. There are no advertising trackers or behavioral analytics.

Exports and deletion

When you export, your browser creates a file on your device. You decide where it goes. You can delete individual facts in the Library. Clearing this site’s browser data removes all local information; export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Licenses

If you buy or restore Rhythm+, the license token is stored in local browser storage and sent to the Sociobot billing API for verification at most once per day. Sociobot / Dodo is the merchant of record and processes checkout details; Retrieval Rhythm does not receive card data.

Offline cache

A service worker caches the app shell and original illustration so the product loads offline. This cache contains application files, not your fact database.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
